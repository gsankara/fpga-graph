### notes that apply to both compile boxes
================================================================================
- Both were created from the SAME `u55c-xilinx` Coder template, so the template
  name says nothing about which card a workspace targets, or whether it has
  cards at all. compile0 and compile3 have none; csg-2fpga has six.
- Both auto-stop after 4 days (`STOPS AFTER 4d`).
- `build_part.sh` gates on `sim/check_wiring.py` before any link: structural +
  9,828 behavioural queries, under a second, no compiler needed.
- Completion is signalled by `BUILD_DONE` written INTO THE LOG plus a
  `BUILD_STATUS_<target>` file. There is no `BUILD_DONE_<target>` file; a
  watcher polling for one waits forever.
- WNS/WHS are read from
  `_xl/reports/link/imp/impl_1_hw_bb_locked_timing_summary_routed.rpt`,
  not from `vpp_link.log`.
